# Lunar New Year Festival 2027 site

This is a Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) site deployed on Vercel. The design is "Option D".

- **Read `DESIGN.md` first.** It covers the tokens, the background-band pattern, the components, the routes and the interactions.
- `reference/*.html` are the visual source of truth. Open them, match them closely, and rebuild them as components. Don't paste the inline-styled markup.
- Assets are in `public/`. Placeholder content is in `data/`.
- Keep the white space and the full-width layout. Use 4px radius on images and cards, and round pills only for buttons and tags. Filter pills use an 8px radius.
- The event-list layout is undecided (`reference/options/`), so keep it swappable.
- Don't show any price UI. Every event is free.
