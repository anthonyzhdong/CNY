The lunar calendar

Lunar New Year isn’t on the same date each year. It follows the lunar calendar, so the new year begins with the new moon and the date changes annually — usually between mid-January and late February.

02

Year of the Sheep

6 February 2027 to 25 January 2028

2027 is the year of the Fire Sheep (sometimes called the Goat). The five elements — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water — rotate through the zodiac, and this year the Sheep is paired with Fire, adding warmth and spark to a famously gentle sign.
