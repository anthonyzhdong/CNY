About

Asian Events Trust (AET) was established to provide a framework to bring the community together to deliver an annual celebration of the Lunar New Year in Wellington.

A mainstay of Wellington’s events calendar, the festival gives local community groups a stage and invites everyone to experience a vibrant, diverse culture.

Connection

Asian and non-Asian audiences connect through an interest and desire to take part, increasing exposure, awareness, understanding and acceptance of Lunar New Year traditions and celebrations.

Ownership

The festival has created a strong sense of ownership within the community, with Asian and non-Asian groups and organisations working together to showcase the cultural diversity of Wellington.

3,500+

volunteers contribute their time, skills and resources each year. They are part of a community that is integral to delivering an authentic celebration of the Lunar New Year.

Our objectives

  1. 01Increase understanding of Lunar New Year tradition and provide opportunities for the wider community to engage with Asian arts and culture.
  2. 02Enliven our streets, places and spaces with enriching arts and cultural experiences that increase understanding of Asian culture.
  3. 03Create pride within the Asian community for their culture and engage youth, creating a pathway for Asian New Zealanders to be actively involved in preserving their cultural heritage.

Our people

The festival organisers are the trustees, and produce the event outside of their paid employment.

Founding Trustee · Chairperson

Linda Lim

An experienced communications, relationships and events manager who has run many successful events outside of the festival, with Dance Aotearoa New Zealand and Victim Support. Linda is a founding Trustee, Chairperson of AET and Programme Director for the festival, and is currently Communications and Engagement Lead for the New Zealand Productivity Commission Te Komihana Whai Hua o Aotearoa.

Sponsorship, community liaison, media and publicity linda@asianeventstrust.co.nz
Trustee · Finance Director

Rita Tom

A qualified Chartered Accountant with wide experience in audit, management and financial reporting. Rita is a Trustee and Accountant for the Trust, and Finance Director for the festival.

Funding and finances rita@asianeventstrust.co.nz

Linda and Rita are supported by Trustee Advisors, who oversee the direction of the festival and help from within their areas of expertise.
